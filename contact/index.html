Frequently Asked Questions

Is Brick Breaker really free?

Yes — completely free. No download, no signup, no in-app purchases. Just open your browser and play instantly on any device.

How do I report a bug?

Use the contact channels above — describe what happened, which mode you were playing, and your browser/device. Bug reports get priority and we typically respond within 1 business day.

Where can I find game tips and guides?

Check our blog for brick breaker guides, strategy tips, and arcade history articles. Each game mode page also has its own FAQ section.

How can I suggest a new game mode?

We love hearing ideas from players — send your suggestion through the Feature Ideas channel above and we will review it. The best community ideas have become real game modes.
