PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: August 7, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Brickbreaker.net does not require user registration or account creation. We do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

We use localStorage in your browser solely to save your high score. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

2. Cookies & Local Storage

Brickbreaker.net uses browser localStorage to persist your game high score. This is not a cookie and contains no personally identifiable information. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

Third-party services (Google AdSense) may set cookies for advertising purposes. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle data.

3. Advertising

We display advertisements via Google AdSense. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the site (pages viewed, time spent, general geographic location). This data is aggregated and anonymous.

5. External Links

Our site may link to external websites (social media, partner game sites). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites.

6. Children's Privacy

Brickbreaker.net is a family-friendly game accessible to all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided personal data through a third-party service on our site, please contact us.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: brickbreaker.net/contact/
