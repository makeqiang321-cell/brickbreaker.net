Play Gun Mayhem 3 Online Free — Full Guide

An arena brawler where the floor is the real enemy.

Gun Mayhem 3 is the updated, action-packed entry in the Gun Mayhem series. It keeps the same core idea as the first two games — blast rivals off floating platforms before they blast you — and layers on more characters, more weapons, and more ways to fight.

The loop is simple but the fights are not. You start with five lives, and every time an opponent knocks you off the stage you lose one. The goal is to shoot, bomb, and knock back the competition until you are the last one standing, or until you have cleared the level's target.

Knockback is the whole game. Guns do not just deal damage — every hit shoves the target backward, and a well-timed shot near an edge is often a one-hit elimination. That is what makes positioning matter as much as aim.

The arsenal is the fun part. There are 29 weapons, from straight shooters to bombs and specials, and each one handles differently. Grabbing a new weapon mid-fight changes how aggressive you can be and how hard you can push someone off the ledge.

You can run it solo against the AI or pass the keyboard to a friend. Death Match Mode is where the multiplayer lives — customize the rules, pick your characters, and build a team for a proper versus.

Being honest about scope: Gun Mayhem 3 is a Flash-era arena brawler built around short, chaotic rounds, not a deep story. The depth is in the roster, the weapons, and the local-versus chaos.

Gun Mayhem 3 Strategy — Fight the Edge

Aim at the platform, not just the player.

Chase the knockback, not the damage. Because every hit shoves the target backward, the highest-value shot is the one near a ledge. Force your opponent toward the edge first, then finish them with a single blast instead of trading damage in the open.

Hold your double jump. The second jump is your recovery tool, and spending it too early leaves you with nothing when a bomb sends you sailing. Keep it in reserve for the moment you are knocked toward the void.

Grab weapons aggressively. The 29 weapons are not equal, and a good pickup mid-fight flips the matchup. When a better gun drops, it is usually worth the risk to contest it rather than keep poking with a weak starter.

Use the bombs for control. Bombs hit around corners and off walls in ways guns cannot, which makes them the best tool for moving an enemy out of a defensive spot. Lobbing one into a camped corner is often a free elimination.

What Makes Gun Mayhem 3 Different

An arena shooter where knockback, not health, decides the fight.

The first difference is the win condition. Most shooters measure damage and health bars. Gun Mayhem 3 measures position — you win by shoving opponents off the stage, so a weak gun used near an edge beats a strong gun used in the open.

The second is the recovery system. The double jump means nobody is ever truly cornered until their second jump is gone, which turns every edge fight into a small game of bait and punish.

The third is the local-versus structure. It is built for one keyboard and up to four people, so the real depth shows up in Death Match, where custom rules and teams turn a simple brawler into a proper couch-competitive game.

Put together, it is an arena shooter that plays more like a fighting game — positioning, knockback, and edge control matter more than raw firepower.

One more round, one more weapon, one more edge kill.

The rounds are short and the rematch is instant. A Gun Mayhem match ends in a minute or two, and losing just means going again with a different weapon draw and a different plan. The loop is tight enough that one more round always feels free.

How do you play Gun Mayhem 3?

Gun Mayhem 3 is a 2D arena shooter where the goal is to knock opponents off the platforms using guns, bombs, and knockback before they knock you off. You have five lives, and every time you get blasted off the stage you lose one. Eliminate the required opponents to clear the level and move on.

What are the controls in Gun Mayhem 3?

Player 1 uses the arrow keys to move and double-jump, Z to fire your primary weapon, and X to throw a bomb. Player 2 uses WASD to move, T to fire, and Y to throw a bomb. Both players double-jump by pressing the jump key a second time in the air, which is how you recover when you are knocked toward the edge.

How many players does Gun Mayhem 3 support?

Gun Mayhem 3 supports local multiplayer on a single keyboard, so you can play solo against AI opponents or with friends sharing one screen. Death Match mode lets you set up custom rules and teams, while Campaign and Survival are built for a solo run against the AI.

What game modes does Gun Mayhem 3 have?

Gun Mayhem 3 has three modes. Survival Mode asks you to eliminate as many opponents as possible to progress. Campaign Mode has you defeat enemy bosses in the arena to unlock additional playable characters. Death Match Mode lets you customize the rules, pick your characters, and build your own team.

How many weapons and characters are in Gun Mayhem 3?

Gun Mayhem 3 includes 18 unique characters, 29 weapons, and 15 levels. Weapons run from straightforward guns to explosives and specials, and every one behaves a little differently, so picking up a new weapon mid-match changes how aggressively you can push an opponent off the edge.

How do you unlock characters in Gun Mayhem 3?

You unlock additional characters by playing Campaign Mode and defeating the enemy bosses in the arena. Each boss you beat adds another playable character to the roster, so the campaign doubles as a way to expand who you can bring into Survival and Death Match.

Does Gun Mayhem 3 work on mobile?

Gun Mayhem 3 is a keyboard-driven Flash game, so it is really built for desktop play with a physical keyboard. It runs in the browser through a Flash emulator, but the arrow-key and WASD controls mean a mouse and keyboard give the intended experience rather than a touchscreen.
