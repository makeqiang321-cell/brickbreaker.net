Play Bloons Tower Defense 4 Online Free — Full Guide

Pop every bloon before it reaches the exit.

Bloons Tower Defense 4 is the classic Ninja Kiwi tower defense game where you defend a winding track with monkey towers. Bloons march along the path in waves, and every one that leaks past your defenses costs you a life. When your lives hit zero, the run is over.

The loop is simple to learn and hard to master. You earn money for popping bloons, spend it between rounds on new towers and upgrades, and start the next wave with the Spacebar. Each tower has a four-tier upgrade path, so a cheap Dart Monkey can grow into a serious defender.

The game ships 9 maps and 3 difficulties. Easy hands you 200 lives across 50 rounds with cheaper towers, Medium drops to 150 lives and 60 rounds, and Hard cuts you to 100 lives and 75 rounds with pricier towers. The harder you go, the less room you have for a leak.

There are 16 towers and weapons to unlock, from the Dart Monkey and Tack Shooter to the Bomb Tower, Ice Tower, Glue Gunner, Monkey Ace, Super Monkey, and the income-generating Banana Farm.

Bloons Tower Defense 4 Strategy — Cover the Track, Grow Your Money

The best defense starts before the bloons do.

Cover the whole track before you chase one big tower. A few cheap towers spread along the path pop more bloons early than a single expensive one, because the bloons move past every section. Start wide, then concentrate your upgrades once the early waves are handled.

Build your income early. The Banana Farm generates money every round, and money now means more towers later. A farm placed in the first few rounds pays for itself many times over by the time the hard waves arrive, so treat it as an investment rather than a luxury.

Match towers to the bloons. Fast bloons need towers that hit reliably, while grouped bloons reward splash damage. Read what each wave is sending and let that decide your next purchase instead of building the same lineup every map.

Upgrade before you add. A well-upgraded tower often outperforms two un-upgraded ones for the same money, so push your best towers up their four-tier path rather than scattering cash across too many weak ones.

What Makes Bloons Tower Defense 4 Different

The tower defense that built a genre.

The first difference is the upgrade depth. Sixteen towers, each with a four-tier upgrade path, means every run is a branching set of choices about what to build and what to push. The same map plays differently depending on which towers you commit to.

The second is the difficulty spread. Easy, Medium, and Hard do not just change the number of rounds — they change your lives, the tower prices, and how much a single leak hurts. The same track becomes a different problem at each setting.

The third is the unlockable modes. Sandbox Mode gives you unlimited money and lives to experiment, and Apopalypse Mode throws endless waves without breaks. They turn a single tower defense campaign into a long-term chase.

How do you play Bloons Tower Defense 4?

Bloons Tower Defense 4 is a tower defense game where you place monkey towers along a winding track to pop waves of bloons before they reach the exit. Every bloon that leaks past your defenses costs you lives, and you lose when they hit zero. Between waves you spend the money you earn on new towers and upgrades to handle bigger and faster bloons.

What are the controls in Bloons Tower Defense 4?

Bloons Tower Defense 4 is mouse and touch controlled. Click or tap a spot on the map to place a tower, and click an existing tower to upgrade it or change its targeting. Press the Spacebar to start the next round and to toggle fast-forward, so the whole game is place, upgrade, and speed up the waves.

How many towers are in Bloons Tower Defense 4?

There are 16 towers and weapons to unlock, including the Dart Monkey, Tack Shooter, Bomb Tower, Ice Tower, Glue Gunner, Monkey Ace, Super Monkey, and Banana Farm. Every tower has a four-tier upgrade path, so the same Dart Monkey can grow from a cheap starter into a much stronger defender as you spend money on it.

How many maps and difficulties are in Bloons Tower Defense 4?

The game has 9 maps and 3 difficulty settings. Easy gives you 200 lives and 50 rounds with cheaper towers, Medium gives 150 lives and 60 rounds, and Hard gives 100 lives and 75 rounds with pricier towers. The harder settings change both how long you must hold out and how little room for error you have.

How do you unlock Sandbox and Apopalypse mode in Bloons Tower Defense 4?

Both extra modes unlock by rank. Sandbox Mode unlocks at rank 26 and gives you unlimited money and lives so you can test tower setups freely. Apopalypse Mode unlocks at rank 31 and throws endless waves at you without the normal breaks between rounds, so it is the endurance challenge for players who have beaten the standard maps.

What is the best tower to use in Bloons Tower Defense 4?

There is no single best tower for every map — the right pick depends on the track and the round. The Dart Monkey is a cheap early game staple, the Super Monkey does heavy damage once you can afford it, and the Banana Farm generates extra money so you can afford more towers sooner. A strong defense usually mixes cheap starters with one or two expensive damage dealers.

Does Bloons Tower Defense 4 work on mobile?

Yes, Bloons Tower Defense 4 runs in the browser and works on touchscreens because the controls are all tap and click based. On a phone or tablet you tap to place towers, tap them to upgrade, and use the on-screen buttons for rounds and fast-forward, so the same strategy carries straight over to mobile without a download.
