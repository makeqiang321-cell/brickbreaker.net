Funny Shooter Strategy — Aim High, Keep Moving

A headshot beats a full magazine.

Aim for the head. Headshots deal far more damage than body shots, so a single clean shot to the head drops an enemy faster than half a magazine into the torso. Slow down for half a second, line it up, and the wave clears faster.

Keep moving. Standing still is how you get swarmed. Strafe while you shoot, and use sprint to create distance while you reload — the gap is your reload timer. Run, turn, shoot, repeat.

Spend your coins on the right weapons. A shotgun for close crowds, a sniper for the heavies, and a rocket launcher for clumped groups. Upgrade one workhorse early rather than spreading coins thin across everything.

Save the explosives. Grenades and launchers are for groups, not singles. A well-placed grenade into a clump of enemies does the work of ten bullets, and wasting it on one grunt is exactly the mistake the harder waves punish.

How do you play Funny Shooter?

What are the controls in Funny Shooter?

Use WASD to move and hold Shift while pressing W to sprint. The mouse aims, Left Click shoots, and Right Click aims down sights. Press Space to jump, R to reload, G to throw a grenade, and T to inspect your weapon. The number keys 1 through 7 switch weapons, and the mouse wheel cycles through what you are carrying.

How do you get new weapons in Funny Shooter?

Every enemy you kill drops coins. Between waves you spend those coins in the shop on new weapons and upgrades — shotguns, snipers, rocket launchers, and grenade launchers — so your damage keeps pace with the tougher enemies. Headshots pay out more, so aim high to build your arsenal faster.

How do you do more damage in Funny Shooter?
