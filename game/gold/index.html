Gold Brick Breaker Scoring Breakdown

Point Values

  • Normal brick: 10 pts — one hit
  • Strong brick: 20 pts — two hits to destroy
  • Gold brick: 50 pts — one hit. Always prioritize these.
  • Level completion bonus: 100 × level number (Level 1 = 100, Level 25 = 2,500)

Gold Brick Breaker Strategy

Targeting Gold Bricks

  • Gold first, always. Identify every gold brick position on level start. Plan your first shot to hit at least one. Don't clear normal bricks until gold is gone.
  • Multi-Ball = score multiplier. Three balls sweeping the screen find gold bricks faster than you can aim. Stack Multi-Ball with Big Paddle for maximum coverage.

Survival & Scoring Balance

  • Bank shots into gold. When a gold brick sits behind normal bricks, use wall ricochets to reach it. Don't clear the blockers — go around them.
  • Don't die chasing gold. A death resets your power-ups and costs you potential gold on future levels. Survive first, score second.
