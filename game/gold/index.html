◆ ABOUT GOLD RUSH

Gold Rush Brick Breaker is all about maximizing your score. Gold bricks appear more frequently in this mode, each worth a massive 50 points — 5× the value of a normal brick. The challenge is reaching them before they're shielded by other bricks or blocked by steel.

Every level is a puzzle: how many gold bricks can you break before clearing the level? The best players target gold bricks first and use multi-ball power-ups to sweep through them. Chase that high score — your personal best is saved automatically.

◆ SCORING GUIDE

  • Normal brick: 10 points — one hit to destroy
  • Strong brick: 20 points — two hits, changes color when damaged
  • Gold brick: 50 points — prioritize these above all else!
  • Level completion bonus: 100 × level number
