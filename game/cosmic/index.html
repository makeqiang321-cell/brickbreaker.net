◆ ABOUT COSMIC MODE

Cosmic Brick Breaker takes you on an interstellar brick-smashing journey. Set against a deep-space starfield with twinkling stars and cosmic particle effects, each level feels like navigating an asteroid field. The cosmic theme transforms the classic breakout experience into something truly out of this world.

As you progress through 25 cosmic levels, the starfield intensifies, new celestial visual effects appear, and the brick patterns evolve from simple constellations to complex galactic formations. Reach level 25 to conquer the cosmos!

◆ COSMIC FEATURES

  • Animated starfield — 80+ stars with randomized twinkle effects
  • Deep space color palette — cosmic blues, nebula purples, and stellar golds
  • Particle trails — balls leave cosmic dust in their wake
  • Level themes — visual effects intensify as you go deeper into space
