Play Vex 2 Online Free — Full Guide

Every act is a puzzle of jumps and timing.

Vex 2 is a stickman platformer that runs on precise movement. You control a nimble stickman through a series of obstacle courses filled with floor and ceiling spikes, spinning saw blades, throwing stars, and moving platforms. One wrong step sends you back to your last flag, and the timer keeps counting.

The controls are tight and few. The Arrow Keys or WASD move you, the Up Arrow or W or Spacebar jumps, and the Down Arrow or S handles the rest — crouching, sliding, and interacting with switches. From that small set grows a deep movement system.

The signature move is the wall jump. Jump into a wall and jump again at the moment of contact to push off, then alternate between two walls to climb straight up. Chained cleanly, it turns vertical walls into ladders and unlocks most of the harder acts.

The game is split into 11 acts, chosen from a central hub. Reach the green portal at the end of an act to unlock the next, and clear the whole campaign to open the stage builder.

Vex 2 Strategy — Keep Momentum, Time the Traps

The fast line is the safe line.

Keep your momentum through every move. Vex 2 rewards a running start, so chain your jumps, wall jumps, and slides instead of stopping to line each one up. The smoother your line, the fewer times you are forced to correct mid-air.

Time the saws and stars, do not race them. The spinning blades follow a rhythm, and the safe pass is to move through the gap right after a blade clears it. Watch the pattern for a moment, then commit in one clean motion.

Use the wall jump to climb, not just to clear gaps. Angle your jumps into a wall to keep your speed and chain upward, because vertical climbing is often the shortest route across a section. The faster you climb, the less time you spend in the path of the traps.

Respect the brittle and bouncy blocks. Purple blocks crumble when you step on them, so keep moving, and orange bouncy blocks launch you high — aim where that launch sends you before you land on one.

What Makes Vex 2 Different

A platformer built out of one move done well.

The first difference is the wall jump. It is not an optional trick — it is the spine of the whole game. Once you can chain jumps between two walls, the vertical sections that look impossible become the fastest part of each act.

The second is the checkpoint trade-off. Flags save your progress but do not stop the timer, so dying costs you time even when it does not cost you progress. That turns every act into a balance between speed and safety.

The third is the movement variety. Alongside the wall jump and slide there are ropes, poles, cannons, zip-lines, and swimming, so no two acts test the same skill. The game keeps introducing a new wrinkle just as you master the last one.

Vex 2 Online — FAQ

Straight answers to what players actually search — how to play, controls, wall jump, slide, levels, checkpoints, and the stage builder.

How do you play Vex 2?

Vex 2 is a stickman platformer where you run, jump, and climb through a series of deadly obstacle courses. Each of the 11 acts ends at a green portal, and reaching it unlocks the next act. Along the way you dodge spikes, spinning saws, throwing stars, and moving platforms, using flags as checkpoints so a mistake only sends you back to the last flag.

What are the controls in Vex 2?

Move with the Arrow Keys or WASD, and jump with the Up Arrow, the W key, or the Spacebar. Press the Down Arrow or S to crouch, to slide while running, or to interact with switches and enter levels. Press M to mute the sound. The whole game is built around a handful of moves chained together.

How do you wall jump in Vex 2?

Jump into a wall and press jump again the moment your stickman touches it to push off and climb. By alternating between two close walls you can chain jumps straight upward, and angling your jumps into a wall keeps your momentum so you can climb faster. It is the core skill that most later acts are built around.

How do you slide in Vex 2?

Slide by pressing the Down Arrow or the S key while you are running. The slide keeps you low so you can pass under low obstacles and gaps that a standing or jumping stickman would clip. It also chains straight out of a sprint, so keep moving when you use it.

How many levels are in Vex 2?

Vex 2 has 11 acts, and you choose them from a central hub. To enter an act you jump onto its block and press the Down Arrow or S. Beating each act by reaching its green portal unlocks the next one, so the campaign opens up one stage at a time.

What do the checkpoints do in Vex 2?

The flags you pass are your checkpoints. When you die, you respawn at the last flag you touched rather than the start of the act, and the level timer keeps running. That makes checkpoints a trade-off: they save your progress but cost you time, so fast runs try to reach them with as few deaths as possible.

How do you unlock the stage builder in Vex 2?

The stage builder unlocks after you complete the game, which means clearing all 11 acts. Once it is open you can build and play your own obstacle courses, so finishing the campaign is the gateway to a second, endless set of levels made by you and other players.
