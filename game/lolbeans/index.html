Play LOLBeans.io Online Free — Full Guide

Thirty beans in, one crown out.

LOLBeans.io is a multiplayer obstacle-course battle royale in the style of Fall Guys. You play a wobbly jelly bean and race up to 30 other players through a course, with the slowest and the fallen cut at the end of each round. Make it through all three elimination rounds and you are the last bean standing.

The controls are simple. WASD or the Arrow Keys move you, the Spacebar or a right-click jumps, and the mouse turns the camera. A dive — Shift or the left mouse button — carries you farther than a jump but locks your direction in the air, so it is a tool for straight gaps, not for course correction.

Every round throws the same threats at you: swinging hammers, spinning bars, slippery slopes, moving and collapsing platforms, bouncing balls, and bottomless pits. Getting hit costs you time, and time is exactly what the qualification cut takes away from you.

Beyond the main race there is a Tag mode for smaller lobbies and a library of community-built custom levels, so the obstacle-course formula keeps giving you new tracks to learn.

LOLBeans.io Strategy — Qualify Smart, Win the Crown

The crown goes to the bean that survives every cut.

Time your jumps past the moving parts. Spinning bars and swinging hammers sweep in a rhythm, and the safe line is to jump so you land past the danger rather than getting clipped mid-jump. Watch the pattern for a beat, then commit — hesitation is what drops you into the pit.

Hug the inside line on curves. The track bends, and cutting the corner shaves distance that adds up over a full round. The shortest path is usually the inside edge, so run it wherever the obstacle layout lets you.

Stay near the middle of the pack. Leaders get targeted and bumped, while stragglers get caught by the cut, so the safe place through the middle of a round is just behind the front. Push for the front only on the final stretch, where position actually decides who qualifies.

Use the dive for distance, not for turns. A dive covers more ground than a jump but locks your direction, so save it for straight gaps where you already know the line. For anything that needs an in-air correction, a normal jump is the safer choice.

What Makes LOLBeans.io Different

A battle royale built out of qualification cuts.

The first difference is the format. It is not one race to a finish line — it is three elimination rounds where the field shrinks every time. You do not need to be fastest overall, only fast enough to stay above the cut, and that changes how you race from the first gate.

The second is the physics. The beans are deliberately wobbly, and bumping and pushing are part of the game rather than an accident. Crowds squeeze, shove, and tip each other over, so position in the pack matters as much as your own timing.

The third is the community content. Beyond the main playlists, players build and share custom obstacle courses, so there is always a new track to learn even after you have mastered the core rounds.

How do you play LOLBeans.io?

LOLBeans.io is a multiplayer obstacle-course battle royale where you play a jelly bean racing up to 30 other players. Each match is split into three elimination rounds, and the beans that finish too slowly or fall get cut at the end of each round. Qualify through all three rounds and you are the last bean standing, which wins the crown.

What are the controls in LOLBeans.io?

Move with the WASD keys or the Arrow Keys, and jump with the Spacebar or a right-click. Move the mouse to turn the camera, hold the left mouse button to look around, and press Shift or the left mouse button to dive. On mobile you steer with a virtual joystick and tap a button to jump, with no download or account required.

How do you qualify in LOLBeans.io?

Each round has a finish line or a qualification zone, and only the first beans to reach it survive. If you are too slow, or if too many other beans have already qualified before you, you are eliminated. The trick is not just finishing — it is finishing inside the qualifying group before the timer or the slot count runs out.

How do you dive in LOLBeans.io?

Dive with the Shift key or the left mouse button. A dive carries you farther than a normal jump, which makes it the right move for crossing a long gap — but you cannot steer while diving, so save it for straight jumps where you know your line and do not need to adjust in the air.

What is Tag mode in LOLBeans.io?

Tag mode is a smaller side playlist of about eight players where one bean chases the rest and being tagged makes you the chaser. It swaps the qualifying race for a chase-and-escape loop, and it sits alongside the community-built custom levels where players create and share their own obstacle courses.

How do you win LOLBeans.io?

You win by surviving all three elimination rounds and being the last bean standing when the final round ends. That means avoiding swinging hammers, spinning bars, slippery slopes, moving platforms, bouncing balls, and bottomless pits, while timing your jumps and pushes so you qualify ahead of the cut in every round.

Does LOLBeans.io work on mobile?

Yes, LOLBeans.io runs in the browser on desktop, tablet, and mobile. On a phone or tablet you move with a left virtual joystick and tap a jump button, so the same race, jump, dive, and qualify loop carries straight over to touchscreens without an app, a download, or an account.
