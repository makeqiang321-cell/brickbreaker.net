Cheat smart, stay invisible, and survive the test.

Cheat or Repeat drops you into a strict classroom with a brutal test and no preparation. You are a desperate student, and cheating becomes the only option — but the teacher is watching, and danger sits in every corner. Suspicion builds through small actions, while hidden clues reveal more than just exam answers.

The core loop is a set of stealth missions. Peek at your phone for answers while monitoring the teacher's movement and minimizing your exposure. Watch the guard patterns and use desks and classmates as cover during risky moments. React to audio warnings by switching views before an inspection catches you out.

Answer execution is the payoff. Once you have found the answers, you input the correct choice fast — balancing speed against caution so you neither make mistakes nor draw attention. The numpad 1 through 4 maps to answers A through D, keeping the whole exchange quick.

The controls spread the tension across your hands. Left mouse reads the answer sheet, right mouse looks around the room, spacebar checks your phone, and W/A/S/D/Q/E search the room based on indicators. Every input is a decision about what to look at, and looking at the wrong thing at the wrong time is how you get caught.

Being honest about scope: Cheat or Repeat is a single-player stealth survival game, not a full school sim. The depth is in the suspicion system — how your choices change the way classmates and authority figures react, and how deep you can push before the pressure breaks.

Cheat or Repeat Strategy — Keep Your Cool

Suspicion is the meter, and short peeks keep it low.

Keep phone usage short. Every second you stay on the phone builds suspicion, so the goal is to grab the answer you need and get back to the paper before the meter spikes. Long peeks are the single biggest way to get caught.

Learn the teacher's route before you risk anything. The guard pattern is predictable, so watch it for a few seconds and time your peeks into the gaps in the patrol rather than gambling mid-route. Desks and classmates are your cover, but only when you are already in position.

Treat audio warnings as a reflex check. When a warning sounds, switch back to your paper view immediately — do not finish the peek, do not grab one more answer. The inspection event is exactly when being on your phone is fatal, so the instant reaction is worth more than the extra clue.

Balance speed and accuracy on the answers. Fast input avoids attention, but a wrong answer wastes the whole risky peek. Once you have the answer confirmed, commit cleanly with the numpad rather than second-guessing under pressure.
