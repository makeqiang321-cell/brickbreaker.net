POWER-UP BRICK BREAKER

◆ MULTI-BALL · BIG PADDLE · EXTRA LIFE ◆

◆ ABOUT POWER-UP MODE

Power-Up Brick Breaker amps up the classic formula with three game-changing abilities that drop randomly when you destroy bricks. Catch them before they fall off screen to turn the tide of any level.

The Big Paddle (W) doubles your paddle width for crucial saves. Multi-Ball (M) splits your ball into three for devastating brick coverage. And the Extra Life (+) gives you a second chance when things go wrong.

◆ POWER-UP STRATEGY

  • Multi-Ball is king — three balls clear bricks 3× faster
  • Don't panic with multiple balls — you only lose a life when the last ball falls
  • Big Paddle makes catching power-ups much easier — chain it with other drops
  • Power-ups stack — you can have Big Paddle AND Multi-Ball active simultaneously
