Play Planet Buster Online Free — Full Guide

One planet, a steady stream of missiles, and a core begging to blow.

Planet Buster is an idle space clicker built around a single satisfying loop: bombard a planet until it detonates. A rotating planet sits in the middle of your screen, wrapped in six colored layers, and your missiles chip away at it one blast at a time. Click to launch, or just hold your cursor over it and let the fire run.

The controls are almost nothing. Press the bomb button to start, tap a missile in the sidebar to swap it, and click upgrade cards to spend your coins. The game does most of the firing for you, so the real work is deciding where the coins go — which is exactly what makes an idle game tick.

Coins come from damage, and damage comes from upgrades. Power makes each missile hit harder, Spawn Rate fires more of them, Gravity and Accuracy help them land, and Offline Earnings keeps the coins coming while you are gone. Earn, reinvest, and watch the next planet fall faster than the last.

Every so often a boss planet appears with a reinforced crust that resists your usual damage. These are the real tests — they take longer and hit back harder, but beating one unlocks new galaxies, new missiles, and new visuals. It is the hook that keeps a simple clicker from going flat.

To be honest about its limits: it is an idle game, so most of it plays itself. The depth is in the upgrade choices and the pacing of boss fights, not in reflexes. But as a low-pressure, high-satisfaction time-filler, it does exactly what it promises.

Planet Buster Strategy — Blow Up Planets Faster

Spend the coins in the right order and the galaxy falls in line.

Prioritize Power and Spawn Rate first. In the early game the fastest way to speed up is more damage per missile and more missiles on screen. Every point in either one directly shortens how long each planet takes, and faster planets mean faster coins, which mean faster everything.

Buy Offline Earnings as soon as it is affordable. It pays for itself while you sleep — coins accrue while you are away, so the first thing you do when you come back is a big round of upgrades. In a game where numbers snowball, catching the offline windfall early compounds hard.

Do not neglect Gravity and Accuracy once planets toughen up. Raw Power stops mattering if the missiles miss, and the later planets are built to punish sloppy fire. A couple of levels here keeps your damage actually landing instead of drifting off into space.

Save a coin buffer before a boss fight. Boss planets have reinforced crusts and take much longer, and having a pile of coins lets you buy a last-second upgrade to break through instead of sitting there grinding. Beat the boss and the new galaxy resets the pace in your favor.

And keep the fire going by holding your cursor over the planet rather than spam-clicking. The game rewards steady pressure over frantic clicking, so settle in and let the loop run.

What Makes Planet Buster Different

A clicker where the target literally blows up in front of you.

The first difference is the visual payoff. Most idle games hide their numbers behind menus, but Planet Buster puts the destruction front and center — you watch a planet's layers peel away one by one until the core detonates. That visible, crunchy feedback is what makes the grind feel earned.

The second is the five-way upgrade split. Rather than one catch-all damage number, Power, Spawn Rate, Gravity, Accuracy, and Offline Earnings each shape how the game feels, so your build actually matters and there is a real order to spend in.

The third is the boss planets. Periodically hitting a reinforced planet that resists your usual fire breaks the monotony and gives you a goal to save toward. Beating one opens a new galaxy, which resets the loop at a higher difficulty instead of just stretching the same numbers forever.

Put it together and you get an idle game that is as nice to watch as it is to leave running — a small but real step up from a generic clicker.

Planet Buster Online — FAQ

Real answers to the questions players actually search — controls, destroying planets, coins, upgrades, bosses, unlocks, and mobile.

How do you play Planet Buster, and what are the controls?

Planet Buster is an idle space clicker built around one simple action: bombard a planet. Click or tap the bomb button to start launching missiles, or just hold your cursor over the planet to keep the fire going. From there you mostly manage the flow — swap missiles from the sidebar and spend coins on upgrade cards while the bombardment keeps running.

How do you destroy a planet in Planet Buster?

Every planet is wrapped in six colored layers, and your missiles chip away at them one blast at a time. Keep the damage up and the layers fall one by one until the core is exposed. Hit the core and it detonates, earning you a big coin payout and moving you on to the next planet — then the cycle starts again with a tougher target.

What do you do with coins in Planet Buster?

Coins are the whole engine of the game. You earn them from the damage you deal, then spend them on upgrades that make your bombardment stronger, faster, and more accurate. The classic idle loop is to earn a pile of coins, reinvest them into the upgrade that helps most, and watch the next planet fall noticeably faster.

What are the upgrade paths in Planet Buster?

There are five upgrade paths: Power, which raises how much damage each missile does; Spawn Rate, which fires missiles faster; Gravity, which helps missiles land; Accuracy on Tap, which sharpens your manual aim; and Offline Earnings, which keeps coins accruing while you are away. Power and Spawn Rate are the early priority, but the other three matter more as planets get tougher.

What are boss planets in Planet Buster?

Boss planets show up periodically with reinforced crusts that are much harder to crack than a normal planet. They take longer and hit back harder, but beating one is how you unlock new galaxies, new missile types, and new visuals. It is worth saving a coin buffer before a boss fight so you can buy a quick upgrade if it is going slow.

How do you unlock new missiles and galaxies in Planet Buster?

New missiles and galaxies unlock as you progress — defeating boss planets is the main gate. Each new galaxy resets the difficulty curve higher and gives you fresh planets to break, while new missile types change how your bombardment feels. Keep upgrading, beat the bosses, and the game keeps opening up new space to wreck.

Can you play Planet Buster on mobile or a phone?

Yes. Planet Buster is an HTML5 game that runs in the browser, and the controls are all taps — so it plays comfortably on a phone or tablet with no download or app. On mobile you tap to bomb, tap missiles to swap them, and tap upgrade cards to spend coins, exactly like the desktop version.
