Play Catdoku Online Free — Full Guide

Catdoku is a cat-themed logic puzzle from ONLYDRK Studio — one cat per row, column, and region, with the aloof "no touching" rule.

Catdoku is Sudoku's cozier cousin. Instead of numbers, you are placing cats, and instead of fixed 3×3 boxes, the board is carved into irregular colored regions. The goal is to put exactly one cat in every row, every column, and every colored region — a clean rule set that turns out to hide a satisfying amount of deduction.

What makes it sing is the "Aloof Rule": cats never touch each other, not even diagonally. Every cat you place instantly rules out the eight cells around it, so the board tightens with every move and the next placement gradually becomes forced. It is logic, not luck — every puzzle has a single solution.

The controls are a two-gesture loop. Click or tap a cell once to mark it with an X and rule it out, then double-click or double-tap to place a cat. Those X marks are your working notes, and the trick is filling in enough of them that the remaining cat placements become obvious.

With over 100 handcrafted levels — some versions run past 300 — and grids that grow from about 8×8 up to 10×10, Catdoku scales its challenge nicely. Daily challenges and optional timed levels add replay value, and a mistake system keeps careful players honest by costing a fish for each wrong placement.

Catdoku Strategy — Deduce, Don't Guess

Catdoku rewards careful deduction, and the fish system punishes careless guesses. Here is how to solve clean.

Target the tightest lines first. Rows, columns, and regions that are nearly full leave the fewest options for their cat, so that is where forced placements live. Whenever a line or region is down to one open cell, its cat is locked in — place it without hesitation.

Squeeze the small regions. Every colored region needs exactly one cat, and a region trapped in a corner often overlaps only one usable cell. When you are stuck, scan the smallest regions on the board; one of them will almost always hand you the next move.

Exploit the Aloof Rule hard. Because cats cannot touch diagonally, a single placement eliminates eight cells at once. Treat each placed cat as a ring of ruled-out cells, not just one filled square, and re-check the neighboring lines and regions every time.

Mark everything you can rule out. Tapping X marks onto impossible cells is not optional busywork — it converts the board into a visual map of what remains, and forced moves that an unmarked board hides become obvious once the dead cells are crossed off.

Be sure before you commit. With the fish-based lives system, a wrong placement costs you, so only double-click a cat when the deduction is airtight. When a board feels ambiguous, you have not found the constraint yet — keep eliminating rather than guessing.

Catdoku Online — FAQ

How to play, the rules, the Aloof Rule, placing cats, level count, wrong placements, and mobile — the quick version.

How do you play Catdoku?

Catdoku is a cat-themed logic puzzle. Each board is divided into rows, columns, and colored regions, and you place exactly one cat in every row, every column, and every region. The challenge comes from the rule that cats cannot sit next to each other, so every placement eliminates the cells around it.

What are the rules of Catdoku?

There are three placement rules in Catdoku: put exactly one cat in each row, exactly one in each column, and exactly one in each colored region. On top of that, cats can never touch each other — not horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Solve each board by deduction, with no guessing required.

What is the Aloof Rule in Catdoku?

The Aloof Rule is Catdoku's name for the no-touching constraint: cats must not be adjacent to each other in any direction, including diagonally. It fits the theme — cats like their space — and it is the key to the puzzle, because placing one cat rules out the eight surrounding cells for every other cat.

How do you place cats and mark cells in Catdoku?

Click or tap an empty cell once to mark it with an X, which rules that spot out. Double-click or double-tap a cell to place a cat in it, and click it again to remove a mark or a placement. Use the X marks to track where cats cannot go, then place a cat in the cell that logic forces.

How many levels does Catdoku have?

Catdoku has more than 100 handcrafted levels, with some versions offering over 300. The difficulty climbs as the grids grow, from around 8×8 on the easier boards up to 10×10 on the harder ones, plus daily challenges and optional timed levels for extra replay value.

What happens when you place a cat wrong in Catdoku?

In versions of Catdoku with a mistake system, a wrong placement costs you one of your lives — shown as fish — and running out ends the attempt, so it pays to be sure before you commit. You can also use in-game hints when you get stuck on a board.

Can you play Catdoku on your phone?

Yes. Catdoku is an HTML5 browser game that runs on desktop, tablet, and mobile, with tap controls that suit touch screens. There is no download, install, or account needed — just open it and start placing cats.
