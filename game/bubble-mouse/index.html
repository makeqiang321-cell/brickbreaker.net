Bubble Mouse wraps a classic bubble shooter around a story: help the mouse blast every bubble and drive the cats out of town.

Bubble Mouse's loop is instantly familiar: a cannon at the bottom, a colored ball loaded, and a board of bubbles above. Aim, fire, and any group of three or more matching bubbles pops. Bubble Mouse Strategy — Beat the Move Limit

Bubble Mouse is won by efficiency, not speed. Here is how to clear more bubbles with fewer shots.

Shoot for the largest same-color cluster you can reach. One shot that pops five bubbles beats five shots that pop one each — and with a move limit hanging over every level, that math is the whole game. Scan the board for the biggest run of your current color before every shot.

Bank shots off the walls for double points. A straight shot stops at the front of the board, but a ball bounced off the side wall curves behind and reaches clusters you cannot hit head-on — and it pays double. Lining up a wall bounce that also lands a big match is the fastest way to run up the score.

Swap with Space before you waste a move. If the loaded ball cannot reach a good match, press Space to bring up the next color. A free swap that sets up a five-bubble clear is always better than spending a move on a two-bubble pop.

Save balls for the bonus round. Finishing a level with leftover bubbles unlocks a timed bonus round for extra coins, so clearing efficiently pays twice — once on the board and again in the bonus. Do not burn shots chasing a perfect clear when a clean clear leaves you more balls to cash in.

Bubble Mouse Online — FAQ

How to play, swapping, the move limit, double points, coins and stars, the bonus round, and mobile — the quick version.

How do you play Bubble Mouse?

Bubble Mouse is a bubble shooter with a mouse-versus-cats story. You fire colored balls from a cannon at the bottom of the screen and match three or more of the same color to clear them from the board. Each level asks you to empty the board within a limited number of moves, and every cleared board helps the mouse drive the cats out of town.

How do you swap the ball in Bubble Mouse?

Press the Space key to swap the ball currently loaded in the cannon with the next one in the queue. Swapping is free and instant, so use it whenever the loaded color cannot reach a match — pulling up the color you need saves a move instead of wasting one.

How does the move limit work in Bubble Mouse?

Every level in Bubble Mouse gives you a limited number of moves, Candy Crush-style, and you have to clear the board before you run out. Because every shot costs a move, the game is won by efficiency: big matches, wall bounces, and dropped clusters clear more bubbles per shot than popping small groups one at a time.

How do you earn double points in Bubble Mouse?

Bounce your shot off the side wall. A ball that rebounds off a wall before it reaches its target is a bank shot, and Bubble Mouse pays double points for it — on top of reaching bubbles a straight shot cannot hit. Lining up a wall bounce that also lands a big match is the fastest way to run up the score.

What are coins and stars for in Bubble Mouse?

Coins are the game's currency: you earn them for big clears and spend them on extra bubbles and bonuses that make harder levels easier. Stars are earned by clearing levels well, and collecting enough stars unlocks the next set of higher levels. Coins buy your way through a tough board; stars prove you cleared it.

How do you unlock the bonus round in Bubble Mouse?

Finish a level with bubbles left over — the game counts your spare balls after the board is clear — and you unlock a bonus round where you pop as much as you can before the timer runs out for extra coins. The cleaner you clear a level, the bigger the bonus opportunity, so saving balls is worth more than a fast clear.

Can you play Bubble Mouse on your phone?

Yes. Bubble Mouse plays in any phone browser with nothing to install. Touch mirrors the mouse — drag to line up a shot, tap to fire, and tap the swap tile to switch the loaded ball. It is an HTML5 build, so iOS and Android need only a browser tab.
