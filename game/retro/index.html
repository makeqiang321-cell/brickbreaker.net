Brick Breaker • Pixel-Art Style • 1976 Tribute

Retro Arcade is our love letter to the brick breaker that started it all — Atari's Breakout from 1976. The color palette pulls straight from the original cabinet: muted brick reds, burnt oranges, harvest golds, and olive greens against a dark field. The HUD uses a monospace font that looks like it belongs on a CRT monitor. No steel bricks, no visual frills, no particle explosions — just the raw, fundamental brick-breaking loop that's been eating quarters for 50 years.

But don't confuse "retro looks" with "retro performance." Under the hood, Retro Arcade runs on the same 60fps engine as every other mode. The paddle responds instantly. The ball physics are identical. You get modern responsiveness wrapped in a vintage aesthetic — like finding a perfectly restored arcade cabinet that plays better than it did in 1976.
