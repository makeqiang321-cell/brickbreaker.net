RETRO BRICK BREAKER

◆ 80S ARCADE TRIBUTE — PIXEL-PERFECT BREAKOUT ◆

◆ ABOUT RETRO MODE

Retro Brick Breaker is our love letter to the arcade era. Inspired by Breakout (1976) on the Atari 2600 — a game co-designed by Steve Wozniak and Steve Jobs — this mode strips away modern complexity and delivers pure, unadulterated brick-breaking satisfaction with a gorgeous retro aesthetic.

The CRT scanline overlay, Press Start 2P pixel font, and neon-glowing bricks recreate the magic of standing in front of an arcade cabinet in 1982. But under the hood, Retro Brick Breaker runs at a buttery 60 frames per second with all the quality-of-life improvements modern players expect.

◆ RETRO GAMING HISTORY

  • Breakout (1976) — The original, designed by Wozniak and Jobs at Atari
  • Arkanoid (1986) — Taito's legendary sequel that introduced power-ups
  • Alleyway (1989) — Nintendo's Game Boy launch title brick breaker
  • DX-Ball (1996) — The classic Windows shareware brick breaker
