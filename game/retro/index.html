◆ ABOUT RETRO MODE

Retro Brick Breaker is our love letter to the arcade era. This mode uses a simplified color palette inspired by the original Breakout (1976) — muted reds, oranges, yellows, and greens against a dark field. Brick layouts follow the classic 5-row grid structure that arcade veterans will recognize instantly. No steel obstacles, no fancy formations — just the raw, fundamental brick-breaking loop.

Under the hood, Retro Brick Breaker runs on the same 60fps engine as every other mode. The ball physics are identical — you get modern responsiveness with a vintage look. The HUD displays score and lives in a clean, minimal layout without the visual frills of other modes. If you want to focus purely on gameplay without any distractions, Retro Mode is for you.

◆ WHAT MAKES RETRO MODE UNIQUE

  • Original Breakout color scheme — muted red, orange, yellow, and green bricks
  • Simplified brick layouts — classic 5-row grids with no steel obstacles
  • Minimal HUD — score and lives only, no level counter or visual effects
  • Standard ball speed curve — no acceleration gimmicks, just steady progression
