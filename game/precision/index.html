◆ ABOUT PRECISION MODE

Precision Brick Breaker rewards accuracy over speed. Bricks are arranged in challenging patterns that require careful angle calculation. Gold bricks appear in hard-to-reach corners, and every shot counts toward your high score. This mode separates the casual players from the true marksmen.

In Precision mode, ball speed is moderate — the challenge comes from brick placement, not frantic reflexes. You have time to line up each shot, but the layouts demand creative thinking. Can you find the perfect angle for every brick?

◆ PRECISION TECHNIQUES

  • Study the brick layout before launching — plan your first shot
  • Use paddle edge deflection for sharp angle shots into corners
  • Bank shots off side walls to reach bricks behind obstacles
  • Target gold bricks first — they're often in the trickiest positions
