Slope Spooky is a 3D rolling-ball endless runner with a Halloween twist — steer down steep haunted slopes and dodge ghostly obstacles.

Slope Spooky is the dark, Halloween version of the classic Slope formula. A monster ball rolls downhill on its own and picks up speed, and your only job is steering left and right to keep it on the path. Simple to pick up, brutal to hold together.

The theme is what sets it apart. Where regular Slope is bright and clean, Slope Spooky runs through dark environments with skull imagery and eerie audio — and the obstacles are ghosts and other Halloween hazards that appear suddenly out of the dark. Every turn grows sharper, and every path feels darker.

The controls are two keys: left and right arrow keys, or A and D. Click "Trick or Treat" to drop in, and the ball never stops on its own — the run only ends when you fall off the path or hit something. Pumpkins scattered along the way unlock features and rewards as you collect them.

There is no finish line. Slope Spooky is endless, which means the real goal is beating your own record and climbing the world rankings. The slope keeps getting faster, so every run is a new high-score chase that ends in a crash you saw coming a second too late.

Slope Spooky Strategy — Survive the Descent

Slope Spooky punishes oversteering and last-second reactions. Here is how to keep the ball on the slope.

Steer gently, not hard. The single biggest cause of death in Slope Spooky is slamming left and right, because at high speed a small overcorrection carries the ball right off the edge. Light, smooth taps are enough to change lanes — let the slope do the work.

Look far ahead. The ghostly obstacles appear suddenly, but the path still telegraphs them if you read it a couple of turns up. Fix your eyes on the road ahead instead of the ball, and you will be dodging before the hazard is on top of you.

Stay toward the center. The middle of the path gives you room to correct in either direction, while hugging the edge leaves you one slip away from falling. Keep the ball in the middle and drift out only to dodge.

Grab pumpkins when they are safe. Pumpkins unlock features and rewards, but chasing one off your line is how runs end. Only reach for a pumpkin when it sits naturally on your path — no pickup is worth the crash.

Keep a rhythm on the sharper turns. As the slope tightens, the steering becomes a rhythm — a gentle lean into each curve, then back to center. Lock into that cadence instead of reacting turn by turn, and you will survive far longer than you expect.

Slope Spooky Online — FAQ

How to play, controls, the difference from regular Slope, starting a run, high scores, crashing, and mobile — the quick version.

How do you play Slope Spooky?

Slope Spooky is a 3D rolling-ball endless runner. A monster ball rolls downhill on its own and picks up speed, and your job is to steer left and right to stay on the path. Ghostly obstacles appear suddenly out of the dark, so you have to dodge them on reaction while the slope keeps getting faster.

What are the controls in Slope Spooky?

Use the left and right arrow keys, or the A and D keys, to steer the ball left and right. That is the whole control scheme — two keys, no jump, no brake. On mobile, tap the left or right side of the screen to steer.

What is different about Slope Spooky compared to regular Slope?

Slope Spooky is the Halloween version of Slope, so it keeps the same rolling-ball downhill gameplay but wraps it in a darker theme — spooky environments, skull imagery, and eerie audio. Obstacles are styled as ghosts and other Halloween hazards that appear suddenly, and pumpkins replace the usual pickups.

How do you start a run in Slope Spooky?

Click or tap the Trick or Treat button to drop in and start the descent. Once the ball is rolling, it never stops on its own — the run only ends when you fall off the path or hit an obstacle, so the descent is one continuous run from start to crash.

How do you get a high score in Slope Spooky?

Steer gently rather than slamming left and right, because oversteering sends the ball off the edge on tight turns. Keep your eyes on the path far ahead so you see obstacles before they reach you, stay toward the center where there is room to correct, and grab pumpkins when they are safe to reach.

Why do I keep crashing in Slope Spooky?

Most crashes come from oversteering or reacting too late. The slopes get faster and sharper the longer you survive, and ghostly obstacles appear suddenly, so a small overcorrection at high speed is usually what knocks you off. Ease off the inputs and read the path a couple of turns ahead instead of steering at the last second.

Can you play Slope Spooky on your phone?

Yes. Slope Spooky is an HTML5 game that runs in any modern browser, and mobile steering works just like the keys — tap the left or right half of the screen to guide the monster ball down the slope. No download, install, or account needed.
