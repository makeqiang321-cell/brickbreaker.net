Play Granny Horror 3 Free — Full Guide

Search, stay quiet, escape in five days.

Granny Horror 3 is a first-person survival horror escape game. You wake up trapped in a mansion with 5 days to get out, and three hunters on your trail: Granny, who hears almost everything, Grandpa, who carries a shotgun, and Slendrina, a ghost who kills you if you look at her.

The game is a giant scavenger hunt. You search rooms, drawers, safes, the cellar, the yard, a shed, a moat, and an abandoned subway station for keys, tools, and parts. You can carry only one item at a time, so you have to learn the house and plan each trip.

Noise is your enemy. Dropping an item or stepping on a creaky floorboard brings Granny running, so you crouch over the creaky spots and hide under beds or in wardrobes when she closes in. Quiet movement is a skill all its own.

There are three ways out: restore the main gate, escape on the underground train, or fly away in a hot-air balloon. Each route needs its own set of parts, and your progress carries over between days, so every attempt pushes you closer.

Granny Horror 3 Strategy — Plan the Route, Move Quiet

Every noise costs you a day.

Pick one escape route and commit. The main gate, the train, and the balloon each need their own parts, and trying to do all three at once just splits your attention. Pick the route whose parts you have found the most of, and focus every trip on finishing it.

Learn the layout before you act. The house is the real puzzle, so spend a Practice run or your first day mapping where the keys, tools, and hiding spots are. Knowing where a wardrobe is before you need it is the difference between escaping and getting caught.

Crouch over the creaky boards. Granny homes in on every sound, and the creaky floorboards are scattered through every hallway. Crouch over them, place items down instead of dropping them, and you can move around the house almost silently.

Never look at Slendrina. The moment she appears, turn away and move. She does not make noise, so the first sign of her is usually visual — and looking at her directly ends the run on the spot.

What Makes Granny Horror 3 Different

Horror built on sound and memory.

The first difference is the three hunters. Granny hears, Grandpa shoots, and Slendrina teleports — each one punishes a different mistake. You are not avoiding one threat, you are managing three, and that keeps the tension from ever settling into a single pattern.

The second is the one-item limit. Carrying only one thing at a time forces you to remember where everything is and plan every trip across the house. The inventory limit turns a simple fetch quest into a memory puzzle.

The third is the persistence. Progress carries over between the 5 days, so failure is not a reset — it is a chance to come back with a plan and a limp. Each day teaches you the house a little better.

Granny Horror 3 — FAQ

Straight answers to what players actually search — how to play, escaping, controls, avoiding Granny, Slendrina, days, and mobile.

How do you play Granny Horror 3?

Granny Horror 3 is a first-person survival horror escape game. You wake up trapped in a mansion and have 5 days to find the items and unlock one of three escape routes, all while Granny, Grandpa, and Slendrina hunt you. You can carry only one item at a time, so you have to remember where everything is and where it goes.

How do you escape in Granny Horror 3?

There are three escape routes. The main gate needs the generator restored, a fuse, and the gate controls. The underground train needs power restored plus the train key, accelerator, and ticket, and a robot guards the station. The hot-air balloon needs the basket, gas or fuel, and burner parts. Any of the three gets you out.

What are the controls in Granny Horror 3?

Move with WASD and look around with the mouse. Press E to pick up or use an item, and hold E to pick a lock. Space drops the item you are holding, C crouches so you cross creaky boards quietly, and R hides under a bed or in a wardrobe. Hold F to escape a bear trap, left-click to shoot, and Esc to pause.

How do you avoid Granny in Granny Horror 3?

Granny hears almost everything. Dropping an item or stepping on a creaky board tells her exactly where you are, so crouch over the creaky spots and place items down gently. When she gets close, hide under a bed or in a wardrobe until she passes. She also sets bear traps, so watch the floor.

Who is Slendrina in Granny Horror 3?

Slendrina is a ghost that teleports and appears suddenly around the house. Looking directly at her kills you, so the moment she appears you have to look away and move. She is one of the three threats alongside Granny and Grandpa, and she is the hardest one to predict because she does not make noise.

How many days do you have to escape in Granny Horror 3?

You have 5 days to escape, and each time you are caught the day ends. The good news is that your progress carries over — installed parts and opened locks stay done — so each day is a chance to push the escape plan further. From day 2 onward you move with a limp, so earlier days matter.

Can you play Granny Horror 3 on mobile?

Yes, Granny Horror 3 runs free in the browser on desktop, mobile, and tablet. On a phone you steer with a virtual joystick and tap on-screen buttons to pick up, use, crouch, and hide, so the same search, avoid, and escape loop carries straight over to touchscreens.
