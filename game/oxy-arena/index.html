A proper little FPS that lives in a browser tab, blocky and fast.

Oxy Arena is a free first-person shooter with blocky voxel graphics that runs entirely in your browser. You drop into a small arena, grab one of five guns, and fight. There is no install and no account — you load the tab and the match is already there, because bots fill any empty slots so the fight starts instead of making you wait for a full lobby.

The gunplay is the point. Five weapons — an AK, SMG, shotgun, sniper, and pistol — each with its own recoil and role. To be straight about the limits: it is a pre-release with a blocky art style, and progress saves locally in your browser, so clearing your data wipes your level and skins. Oxy Arena Strategy — Win More Fights

Keep the crosshair at head height, learn the maps, and reload behind cover.

Aim for the head from the start. Headshots are the difference between winning and losing a straight 1v1, because the extra damage means the other player is dead before they can react. Keep your crosshair at head height as you move instead of pointing it at the floor, and you will walk into fights already half-aimed.

Learn where the power spots are. Each of the six maps has a couple of positions that are simply better — a high angle, a long sightline, a corner with an escape. Knowing those spots means you can pre-aim them and catch people moving through. Spend a few rounds in Practice just learning the geometry before you take it into Deathmatch.

Do not sprint into the open. Sprint is for rotating between cover, not for entering a fight, because you cannot shoot while sprinting and your crosshair is off. Move to a corner, clear it, then move to the next. Players who sprint around corners are the easiest kills in the lobby.

Reload behind cover. The AK and SMG burn through a mag fast, and reloading in the open is a death sentence. After every kill or near-miss, break line of sight before you press R. And use Backrooms: Zombies as a warm-up — five waves of zombies is the cheapest way to get your aim and recoil control dialed in before you face real players.

Oxy Arena Online — FAQ

Real answers to the questions players actually search — controls, multiplayer, modes, maps and guns, accounts, private rooms, and mobile.

How do you play Oxy Arena, and what are the controls?

Oxy Arena is a first-person shooter: move with WASD, look around with the mouse, and click to fire. Left-click shoots, right-click aims down sights, Space jumps, Shift sprints, C crouches, R reloads, and the number keys 1 through 5 switch your gun. Esc opens the menu. Headshots deal extra damage, and aim-down-sights tightens your spread, so the skill is in crosshair placement and recoil control, not just reflexes. On mobile there are touch controls, but a mouse is strongly recommended for an FPS.

Is Oxy Arena multiplayer, and how many players can join a match?

Yes, Oxy Arena is primarily multiplayer. Deathmatch and Team Deathmatch support up to 8 players, and team battles are 4v4. The smart part is that bots fill any empty slots, so a match starts right away even if the lobby is not full — you are never stuck waiting for eight humans to show up. You can also jump into the solo Backrooms mode if you want a break from fighting people.

What game modes does Oxy Arena have?

Oxy Arena has Deathmatch, Team Deathmatch, and a solo mode called Backrooms: Zombies. Deathmatch is every player for themselves, Team Deathmatch is 4v4, and Backrooms: Zombies is a five-wave survival fight against zombies that is good for warming up your aim without the pressure of other players. There is also a Practice mode and private rooms where you control who is in the match.

What maps and guns are in Oxy Arena?

There are six maps — Downtown, Ruins, Orbital (a space station), Dungeon, Block Fort, and the Backrooms — and five guns: an AK, an SMG, a shotgun, a sniper, and a pistol. Every gun has its own recoil and role, from the SMG for close range to the sniper for holding long sightlines. The maps are blocky voxel arenas, so the geometry is simple enough to learn fast, and each one has a couple of spots that are clearly better than everywhere else.

Do you need an account to play Oxy Arena, and is it free?

No account is needed — Oxy Arena is free and you just load it and play. Your progress, which includes your level, gun skins, and daily challenges, is saved locally in your browser rather than to a server account. That convenience has one catch: clearing your browser data will wipe your level and skins. There is no download and no payment, just a browser tab and a match.

How do you create a private room in Oxy Arena?

From the menu, choose to create a room and the game gives you a room code or invite link to share with friends, plus an optional password. Anyone with the code and password joins your room, so you can set up a private Deathmatch or Team Deathmatch with just the people you want. It is the cleanest way to play against friends without a random matchmaker putting you together.

Can you play Oxy Arena on mobile?

Yes, Oxy Arena has touch controls and runs in a mobile browser, but it is honestly a worse experience on a phone. A first-person shooter wants a mouse for aiming, and the on-screen buttons cover too much of a small screen to track targets well. It is fine for a quick casual match, but if you want to actually compete, play it on desktop with a mouse. The game is designed as a desktop FPS first.
