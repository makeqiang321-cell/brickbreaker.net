Play City Escape 2 Online Free — Full Guide

Outrun the city, one rooftop at a time.

City Escape 2 is a stickman parkour runner that throws you onto the rooftops with the police close behind. Your hero runs forward on their own, and everything else is on you — the jumps, the vaults, and the split-second reads that keep the chase alive.

The controls are small but deep. The Up Arrow or W key jumps, and holding the button longer gives you a higher leap, so the same key produces a low hop or a full launch depending on the press. The Right Arrow or D key is your vault, triggered when a purple icon flashes above your head as an officer or pedestrian blocks the path.

The roof is your lifeline. Fall to the street and you have to scramble back up before the police close in, so the run is about staying high, landing clean, and reading the gaps before you reach them.

The signature move is the springboard. Press jump right before running into someone and instead of tripping, you launch way into the air — the tool that turns a bad landing into a recovery and an ordinary jump into an escape.

City Escape 2 Strategy — Read the Roof, Ride the Vault

The escape is in the timing.

Hold the jump on purpose. A tapped jump is a low hop and a held jump is a launch, so measure every gap before you press. The wider the gap, the longer you hold — the same key does different work depending on the press.

Watch for the purple icon. The vault cue flashes the moment before you reach an officer or a pedestrian, and hitting Right or D on that flash turns a collision into a boost. Late means you trip, early means you waste the move, so the flash is the whole game.

Springboard instead of falling. When you are about to run into someone, press jump right before impact to launch upward. It is both a recovery and an offensive move — the cleanest way to climb back onto a high roof or clear a blocker without losing speed.

Get off the ground fast. Falling to the street is not death, but it is a countdown, because the police gain ground while you are down. Your first priority after a fall is finding the quickest line back up — a ledge, a springboard, or a held jump.

What Makes City Escape 2 Different

A runner where the obstacles are people.

The first difference is the pressure. This is not a distance run against a timer — it is a chase, with the police always behind you. That turns every stumble into a genuine threat and every clean vault into a small escape.

The second is the two-move depth. Jump and vault look simple, but the held-jump height and the purple-icon timing mean the game rewards precision rather than speed alone. Two keys, and the whole skill is in when you press them.

The third is the springboard. Jumping right before impact converts a collision into a launch, so the obstacles you would normally dodge become your way back up. It flips the usual runner rule — sometimes the thing in your path is your best move.

Put together, it is a rooftop chase that feels tense, reads fast, and rewards timing over raw reflexes.

How do you play City Escape 2?

City Escape 2 is a stickman parkour runner where your hero runs forward on their own, and you control the jumps and vaults as the police chase you across the city rooftops. You jump from building to building, dodge pedestrians who trip you, and vault over cops to keep the chase going. The goal is to keep moving, stay off the ground, and get as far as you can without being caught.

What are the controls in City Escape 2?

City Escape 2 is a keyboard game. Press the Up Arrow, the W key, or the south gamepad button to jump, and press the Right Arrow or the D key to vault over police officers and pedestrians when the purple warning icon appears above your head. The Space key returns to the main menu, and the jump height depends on how long you hold the button.

How do you jump higher in City Escape 2?

Your jump height depends on how quickly you release the button, so holding the jump key longer gives you a higher leap. That is how you clear the wider gaps between buildings and get back up onto the rooftops. A short tap is a low hop, and a held press is a full launch.

How do you vault over cops in City Escape 2?

When a police officer or a pedestrian is directly in your path, a purple icon flashes above your hero. That is the vault cue — press the Right Arrow or the D key at that moment to vault over them and boost yourself forward instead of crashing into them.

How do you do a springboard jump in City Escape 2?

You do a springboard jump by pressing the jump button right before running into someone. If you time it correctly, instead of tripping you launch way up into the air, which is how you reach high rooftops and recover when a building is out of reach.

What happens if you fall off a building in City Escape 2?

If you fall to the ground you do not instantly lose — but you need to get back up onto the rooftops as fast as you can, because the police close in while you are down. Look for something to jump onto or a springboard target to launch yourself back up before the chase catches up.

Does City Escape 2 work on mobile?

No, City Escape 2 is a keyboard-only game, so it is built for desktop play in the browser. It uses the arrow keys or WASD plus the Spacebar, which means there is no touch control for phones or tablets. Play it on a computer with a keyboard for the intended experience.
