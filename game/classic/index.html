◆ ABOUT CLASSIC MODE

Classic Brick Breaker brings back the original breakout experience that started it all. Simple brick layouts, steady ball speed, and pure arcade satisfaction — just like the 1976 original, but with modern neon visuals.

This mode features standard brick patterns with all 4 brick types (Normal, Strong, Gold, Steel). Perfect for beginners learning the basics of angle control, or veterans who want that authentic retro feel.

◆ TIPS FOR BEGINNERS

  • Stay near the center of the screen for best paddle coverage
  • Hit the ball with paddle edges to create angled shots
  • Prioritize gold bricks — they're worth 5× normal points
  • Use arrow keys or A/D keys for precise paddle movement
