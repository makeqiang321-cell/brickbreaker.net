◆ ABOUT NEON STYLE

Neon Brick Breaker turns every level into a dazzling retro light show. Bricks glow with pink, cyan, gold, and purple neon effects. The cosmic starfield background and CRT scanline overlay create an authentic 80s arcade atmosphere that honors gaming's golden age.

Every brick destruction triggers a burst of colored particles. The paddle leaves a subtle cyan trail. Score numbers float upward in neon gold. It's the same addictive gameplay you love, wrapped in one of the most visually distinctive browser game designs on the web.

◆ VISUAL FEATURES

  • 4 neon brick colors — pink, cyan, gold, and purple with CSS glow effects
  • Particle explosions on every brick destroyed
  • CRT scanline overlay for authentic retro arcade feel
  • Animated starfield background with twinkling stars
