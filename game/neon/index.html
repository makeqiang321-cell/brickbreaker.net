◆ RETRO ARCADE GLOW — COSMIC VISUALS ◆

◆ ABOUT NEON STYLE

Neon Brick Breaker amplifies the visual intensity of every moment. Particle effects are doubled — each brick destruction explodes with extra sparkles in the brick's own color. The starfield background is denser and brighter, creating a cosmic light show behind the action. HUD elements pulse gently with a glow effect that intensifies as your score climbs.

This mode uses the same core physics and brick layouts as Classic, but every visual system runs at maximum intensity. If you love watching bricks shatter in a burst of color, Neon Style is your mode. The score float animations are larger, the power-up pickups flash more dramatically, and even the paddle leaves a subtle motion trail as it moves.

◆ WHAT'S DIFFERENT IN NEON MODE

  • Double particle count — every brick hit produces twice the visual feedback
  • Enhanced color palette — bricks use vibrant purple, electric blue, gold, and violet
  • Paddle motion trail — a subtle afterglow follows your paddle movement
  • Pulsing HUD — score and level indicators glow brighter as you progress
