Play Krillion Online Free — Full Guide

Krillion is a daily trivia and word game with reverse scoring — the rarer your answer, the more points it is worth.

Krillion flips trivia upside down. Instead of picking the right answer from a list, everyone gets the same 7 open-ended prompts each day — "name a cheese," "name an actor," "name a spice" — and you have 25 seconds per prompt to type one valid response. There is no multiple choice, and there is no "correct" answer. Anything valid counts.

The twist is reverse scoring. Points are not awarded for knowing the answer, but for being rare. If you answer "cheese" with "cheddar," you sink to the Plankton tier for 10 points — because everybody thought of cheddar. If you pull out "halloumi" or "taleggio," you climb through the tiers toward the 100-point One in a Krillion.

Your score converts into ocean depth, so a strong run sinks you deeper toward the Trench floor at 2,800 meters, and at the end you get a shareable strip that shows how far down you went. The whole loop takes a couple of minutes, which is exactly why people come back every single day.

There is no account, no download, and no ads. The Daily Dive resets at 00:00 UTC with seven fresh prompts, so every player is answering the same new questions on a level field — and the race to think of the most obscure valid answer starts over.

Krillion Strategy — Think Rare, Answer Fast

Krillion rewards obscurity, but the 25-second timer punishes overthinking. Here is how to sink deep.

Skip your first instinct. Your first answer is almost always the most common one — "cheddar" for cheese, "apple" for fruit — which is exactly what lands you in the Plankton tier for 10 points. Give yourself a beat to reject the obvious answer and reach one level deeper.

Go specific, not obscure-for-its-own-sake. The goal is still a valid answer, so a rare but real response beats a nonsense string that gets rejected. Think of a real example a casual player would not name — a regional cheese, a supporting actor, a spice from a specific cuisine — rather than guessing wildly.

Mind the timer. 25 seconds is enough to think of a good answer, but not to agonize. Lock in a solidly rare response by the 15-second mark so you never finish a prompt with nothing, because a zero is worth less than any valid answer.

Warm up before the Daily Dive. Krillion has a free practice prompt, so run a few answers there to get your brain into "rare but real" mode before you touch your actual daily score.

Lean on themed packs. The movies, sports, geography, and pop-culture packs reward depth in niches you actually know, so a category you follow closely is where you are most likely to pull a Deep Cut or One in a Krillion answer.

Krillion Online — FAQ

How to play, reverse scoring, One in a Krillion, max score, the daily reset, game modes, and mobile — the quick version.

How do you play Krillion?

Krillion is a daily trivia and word game built around obscurity. Every day everyone gets the same 7 open-ended prompts — like "name a cheese" or "name an actor" — and you have 25 seconds per prompt to type one valid answer. There is no multiple choice, so the trick is answering with something correct but rare.

How does Krillion scoring work?

Krillion uses reverse scoring, which means the rarer your answer is among all players, the more points you get. A common answer lands in the Plankton tier for 10 points, while a rare one climbs through Too Clever, Schooler, Rare, and Deep Cut — all the way to One in a Krillion for 100 points. Your final score becomes an ocean depth, from the Shallows down to the Trench.

What is a One in a Krillion in Krillion?

One in a Krillion is the top scoring tier in Krillion, worth 100 points. You reach it by answering a prompt with a valid response that almost nobody else gave that day. It is the game's way of rewarding the most obscure correct answer you can think of before the 25-second timer runs out.

How many points can you score in Krillion?

The maximum score in a single Daily Dive is 700 points — 7 prompts at 100 points each. Getting there means landing a One in a Krillion answer on every single prompt, which is extremely rare, so a strong real-world score is usually a few hundred points spread across the middle and upper tiers.

When does the Krillion Daily Dive reset?

The Daily Dive resets at 00:00 UTC every day. When it resets, everyone receives a fresh set of 7 prompts, so every player is answering the same new questions on an even playing field — and yesterday's rare answers become worthless once the new dive begins.

What game modes does Krillion have?

Krillion has the Daily Dive as its main mode, plus themed packs built around topics like movies and TV, sports, geography, and pop culture. There is also an Infinite mode for endless prompts, and a free practice prompt so you can warm up without touching your daily score.

Can you play Krillion on your phone?

Yes. Krillion is an HTML5 game that runs in any modern browser on desktop, tablet, and mobile, and it uses a simple keyboard or on-screen input for typing answers. There is no download, account, or install required, and the game is free with no ads.
