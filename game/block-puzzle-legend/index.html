Block Puzzle Legend Tips — Clear Smarter, Not Faster

Block Puzzle Legend rewards patience and spatial awareness. Here is how to push your score higher.

Clear from the bottom up because when you clear a low row, everything above it drops down — that cascading shift opens up new placements you did not have a second ago. Top-down clearing does nothing for you; it just leaves gaps in worse positions.

Leave breathing room because a tight, packed board kills runs fast. Keep at least one open vertical lane so large pieces have somewhere to land. Stuff every corner and you are out of moves before you know it.

Target the pre-placed rows early because those near-complete rows the game starts you with — the ones at 8/10 or 9/10 — are free points waiting to happen. Clear them in your first few moves to buy space and momentum when both matter most.

Chain your clears for big scores because one row at a time is survival mode, but two or three rows with a single piece is how you climb the leaderboard. Scan for the shape that can finish multiple rows at once — that piece is worth more than any other on your tray.

Do not fall in love with the center because new players gravitate toward the middle of the grid while experienced players spread out. Use the edges, use the corners, and keep the board balanced — lopsided boards collapse on the heavy side every time.
