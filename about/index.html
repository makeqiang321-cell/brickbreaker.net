ABOUT BRICKBREAKER

A passion project built by retro gaming enthusiasts, for retro gaming enthusiasts.

◆ OUR MISSION

Brickbreaker.net exists for one reason: to bring the pure, addictive joy of brick-breaking arcade games to everyone, everywhere — completely free, with no downloads, no sign-ups, and no paywalls. We believe the best games are the ones you can start playing in seconds, and our browser-based brick breaker delivers exactly that.

We're inspired by gaming history — from Breakout (1976) on the Atari 2600, co-designed by Steve Wozniak and Steve Jobs, to Arkanoid (1986) by Taito, which introduced power-ups and boss battles to the genre. Brickbreaker.net is our tribute to that legacy, rebuilt for the modern web with a neon retro aesthetic that honors the arcade era.

◆ WHY WE BUILT THIS

In 2026, the free web gaming space is dominated by ad-heavy portals with hundreds of low-quality games. We wanted to do the opposite: one game, done really well. Brickbreaker.net focuses exclusively on the brick-breaking genre we love, with a level of polish and attention to detail that most browser games lack.

Our game features 60fps Canvas rendering, Web Audio sound effects, particle explosions, 4 brick types, 3 power-ups, progressive difficulty, and persistent high scores — all running in a single, self-contained HTML file. It's lean, it's fast, and it's built to last.
